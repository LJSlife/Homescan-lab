# HomeScan 3.0.0 — clarity reset

The user-facing scan and OCR workflows have been removed from the Add Item experience because the local recognition stack did not meet a dependable quality bar. The product now prioritises a clear manual-first record flow.

## Add Item
A single scrolling form replaces the wizard and tabbed editor. Only the item name is required. Product identifiers, category, quantity and photos are optional.

A product-label photo can be saved as a reference, but HomeScan 3.0.0 does not claim to extract values from it. Unknown information stays blank.

## Extras
Manuals/warranty/care and Documents/notes are collapsed by default so they do not compete with the primary task.

## Room
The Camera Assist tab is removed. The room screen is an item list plus one clear + Add Item action.
