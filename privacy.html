Your information

What this build uses and where it stays. Updated 13 October 2026.

Home records and memories

Room records, photos, videos, documents and notes stay in this browser on this device. Recognition models download from external providers; this app processes the photos on your device. Household records and memories do not automatically sync to a server or another family member.

External information links open only when selected. Sharing a memory opens your phone’s share options; you choose the destination.

Gmail and contacts

You choose a Google account and give permission. The app fetches selected inbox messages, their sender/subject/text and attachment details directly from Google. Summaries select key sentences on this device; email text is not sent to an AI provider. Checking does not mark your emails read.

Google Contacts and Other contacts load when you request them. Contacts, drafts and kept attachments are saved locally for the selected account. Original attachment bytes can be downloaded and backed up. They are not uploaded to HomeScan hosting.

Google access tokens remain in memory until expiry, reload or disconnection and are not saved by HomeScan. After you have connected once, HomeScan can ask Google for a fresh short-lived token when you return, using the existing grant and saved account hint; Google may still require you to tap Reconnect Gmail. No password or token is included in an app backup. Disconnect disables automatic reconnection and stops this app’s API requests but keeps your local files and drafts. You can remove Google's permission at your Google account connections.

You explicitly review and send each message. The recipients receive its contents through Gmail. Gmail’s normal service and retention settings continue to apply. There is no background inbox monitoring in this build.

Voice

Speech recognition is provided by your browser or phone. It may send audio to its speech service. Check the transcript before applying it. Voice does not automatically send emails.

Local storage and backups

This app does not provide a separate local account lock or encryption layer. Use your own device. Clearing website data can remove saved records, files and drafts. Keep backups outside this browser. Home records, Memories and Email attachments have separate backups.

Removing an attachment's saved copy does not remove the original from Gmail. Backups contain private information; share them only where intended.

Hosting and third-party services

GitHub Pages hosts the app’s public code and receives normal website requests. Google handles Gmail authorisation and API requests. Model/library hosts receive download requests. Their own privacy policies apply to their services. This app code does not contain advertising, analytics tracking or an inbox content collection server.

Before public release

This page describes the supplied personal-testing build. The app owner must provide accurate owner/support contact information and complete Google's applicable registration and verification before public distribution. This page alone is not evidence of Google's approval.
